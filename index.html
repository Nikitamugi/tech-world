Who We Are

Over a Decade of Delivery

Tech-World Africa Limited was incorporated on 13th January 2014 under the Companies Act, Cap 486 of the Laws of Kenya. We specialise in mechanical, building and civil works, road works and general installations — delivering affordable, high-quality services to clients across the country.

Our teams of highly trained professionals work in conjunction with consultants to design, supervise and implement projects at minimum cost and maximum efficiency. We advise clients in all aspects of the project and guide them from conception to completion — endeavouring to achieve the highest level of Civil and Building works standards in project execution, supervision and on-site quality control.

People

We recruit knowledgeable and highly skilled professionals while providing ongoing opportunities in training and self-development.

Commitment

Our commitment to quality and timely delivery has established us as one of the leading engineering and construction firms in the region.

Integrity

A reputation built on honesty and trust — clients return to us repeatedly for their most important projects.

Safety

An independent OH&S consultant conducts regular jobsite inspections and safety audits to ensure compliance at all levels.

Adaptability

All projects are custom-built and tailored to meet specific requirements — adjusting construction specifications when needed.

Continuous Learning

We integrate the latest construction and engineering technology so our projects are never compromised by disruption or obsolescence.

Customer Satisfaction

Our work is never complete without the client's final approval — only after thorough inspection and testing is any project handed over.

Environmental Care

We concentrate on minimising environmental impact, ensuring good air quality and minimisation of noise pollution on all sites.

Our Mission

To meet and exceed our clients' expectations — fostering long-term partnerships.

We believe honesty and integrity shall be instilled in every job. We work hard for our clients to ensure the best prices and best quality work. We collaborate with the best subcontractors to bring your project in on time, under budget, and to the best quality workmanship.

Our Vision

To be the leading company of choice for Building and Civil Engineering services in Kenya and the East and Central African Region.

We believe our employees are our best asset — working hard every day to complete each job with quality workmanship, attention to detail, and pride. Tech-World Africa has the equipment and skilled workforce to handle any project, however extensive.
