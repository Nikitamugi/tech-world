Water Works & Irrigation · Kajiado County

Rehabilitation of Nguruman Irrigation Scheme

Client: Ministry of Water, Sanitation & Irrigation Contract No: MWSI/SDI/ONT/OIT3/2023–2024 Start: 16 Jan 2024  |  End: 16 Feb 2026 Commissioned: 3 February 2026
Waterworks & Irrigation Building Construction Earthworks Pipeline Installation Drainage Works

Tech-World Africa Limited was contracted by the State Department for Irrigation under the Ministry of Water, Sanitation & Irrigation to carry out the full rehabilitation of the Nguruman Irrigation Scheme in Kajiado County. Our teams of highly trained professionals worked in conjunction with the client's consultants to design, supervise and implement all project components at minimum cost and maximum efficiency.

Works encompassed the full scope of our civil and building capabilities: large-diameter HDPE pipeline installation, excavation and earth pan works, canal and valve chamber construction, scheme management buildings, ablution facilities, and a fully operational sprinkler irrigation distribution system — all delivered through stringent on-site quality control. The project was officially commissioned on 3rd February 2026 by CPA Ephantus Kimotho, CBS, Principal Secretary, State Department for Irrigation.

Water Works & Irrigation · Central Kenya

Githuya, Riakomo & Ndakaini Irrigation Projects

Type: Gravity-Fed Irrigation Scheme Works Works Period: November 2021 Scope: Intake Weir, Sedimentation Tank, GI Pipework, Manholes
Waterworks & Irrigation Intake Weir Construction Sedimentation Tank GI Pipe Installation Manhole Chambers Trench Excavation Air Valve Installation

Tech-World Africa Limited carried out water supply and irrigation infrastructure works across the Githuya, Riakomo, and Ndakaini Irrigation Schemes in Central Kenya. The scope of works spanned the full pipeline corridor from source to distribution — including construction of intake weirs, sedimentation tanks, trench excavation through densely forested hillside terrain, installation of GI pipes and fittings, manhole chamber construction, and installation of air valves and pressure control fittings.

Works were carried out in challenging steep-gradient and forested conditions, requiring skilled manual excavation for trench formation, careful pipe bedding and jointing, and concrete anchor block construction to secure the pipeline across gradient changes. Pressure testing was conducted on completed sections to verify system integrity before backfilling and commissioning.

Earthworks · Kajiado County

Earthworks & Water Pan Construction — Kalema

Location: Kalema, Kajiado County Date: September 2024 Type: Excavation & Earth Dam Works
Excavation & Earthworks Earth Dam / Water Pan Stone Pitching Compaction

Excavation and construction of a water pan in Kalema, Kajiado County. Works included mass earthmoving, stone pitching of embankments, spillway formation, base compaction, and concrete apron works. Our company specialises in the excavation of earth dams and pans — a core civil works capability supported by experienced site engineers, foremen, and a dedicated on-site workforce.

The project involved close collaboration between our site team and the client's supervising engineers to ensure embankment geometry, compaction specifications, and spillway levels were met precisely.
